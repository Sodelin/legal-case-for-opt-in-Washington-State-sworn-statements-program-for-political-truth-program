**Entered:** 2026-08-30

## Order

Candidate v1.1 has a defensible core: a voluntary, in-person, Washington-only, tangible Canonical Sworn Record (CSR), with the ordinary interview and Public Media Record remaining unsworn. It cures the most serious earlier error—treating a whole oral “sworn block” as the oath object.

Neither live pull request may be treated as merge-ready or operational. The project remains in Phase 0 synthetic/tabletop testing. No named participant, publication, livestream, remote act, public refusal label, or criminal-enforcement claim is authorized.

## Findings sustained

1. Washington notaries may administer oaths generally, and Washington law recognizes verification on oath of a statement in a record.
2. The exact paper CSR may be the sole sworn object; transcript, PMR, source packet, annotations, and later commentary may remain unsworn.
3. False swearing is a plausible but untested theory on these private facts.
4. First-degree perjury is colorable but unresolved; no located controlling case makes every private notarization an official proceeding.
5. Pre-freeze participant-controlled correction and a completely new act after execution are sound controls.
6. A nonpublic, in-person, wet-ink feasibility phase is the present conservative design.
7. Nonpublication of identified invitee/nonparticipant status through Phase 2 is required.

## Findings rejected or qualified

1. A notary does not certify truth, proposition content, legal sufficiency, capacity, competence, or voluntariness.
2. The notary must not approve proposition admission, source selection, record wording, or certificate interpretation.
3. Outside text cannot be incorporated by reference while the CSR is called the only sworn object. Reproduce or physically append adopted text inside the CSR, or keep it unsworn.
4. RCW 10.88.250 does not govern Washington’s demand for a signer located elsewhere; it governs Washington surrendering a person found in Washington to another state.
5. Correct *Pearsall-Stipek* to 141 Wn.2d 774–78 for false swearing and 779 for retraction. Clarify that *Lewis*, 85 Wn.2d 769, involved the notary’s absence and a fact-limited irregularity holding.
6. Delete unsupported forecasts about prosecution, charging, surrender, or an interstate “fight.” State the legal gates only.
7. DGG’s stricter signed pre-consent policy is valid, but Washington law also recognizes a recorded announcement; do not misstate the statute.
8. “Viewpoint-neutral” is a project/editorial/study rule, not a private First Amendment obligation.
9. FEC AO 2008-14 is a fact-bound analogy, not a blanket Internet-press safe harbor.
10. A disclaimer or audience test does not immunize a false or defamatory implication.
11. Existing oath research does not prove political speakers will become truthful. The 2025 21-condition megastudy and adverse transport evidence must be included.
12. Observed 90% comprehension, nonsignificance, and zero incidents do not prove safety.

## Findings for subsequent reviewers

- The legacy pilot packet v2, section 5.3, says a Washington notary is only “Ideally” physically in Washington. [WAC 308-30-110](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=308-30-110&pdf=true) requires physical presence within Washington. The later operating materials already state this.
- The legacy packet's Lewis citation is incorrect. The correct citation is [State v. Lewis, 85 Wn.2d 769, 539 P.2d 677 (1975)](https://law.justia.com/cases/washington/supreme-court/1975/43613-1.html). It concerns a DMV affidavit under former law, not this media format.
- The supplied August 29 Word memorandum's introductory “constitutional safe harbor” wording exceeds its later qualification that criminal prosecution remains government action. The September PDF explicitly preserves the distinction.
- [RCW 9A.72.080](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=9A.72.080), concerning unqualified assertions without knowledge, appears in later materials but is absent from several older summaries.
- Publication rules for identified nonparticipants differ between the supplied August 30 protocol and this repository's operating packet section 9. The full review records the exact passages; it does not adopt a replacement publication rule.
- The latest scope description identifies a separate organization with participation available across parties. Earlier DGG-as-operator materials and the newer description do not yet share an adopted governance instrument. Possible recipients are not confirmed partners.
- The 2025 honesty-oath megastudy is already discussed in the September PDF, although absent from the older behavioral MD. Related experiments do not establish an effect for this exact notarial political-interview format.
- A previously inaccessible [2026 correction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42372171/) has now been retrieved in full (DOI 10.1073/pnas.2610441123; PMCID PMC13342925). The direct replication's corrected average-performance effect is d = +0.04, 95% CI [-0.07, +0.15], n = 1,235. The reported failure to detect a signing-first effect remains. This concerns signature placement, not all honesty oaths.
- [RCW 42.45.280](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=42.45.280) still requires separating current text from the replacement effective January 1, 2027. The enacted future subsection is not current authority.
- Notarial recognition, criminal jurisdiction, offense elements, evidence admissibility, and empirical effectiveness remain separate inquiries.

# Independent paralegal authority audit and implementation handoff

**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-30  
**Project:** Digital Ground Game (DGG) voluntary sworn-factual-record concept  
**Status:** Public-safe research audit; not legal advice, launch clearance, a participant form, or a prediction of prosecution  
**Decision vocabulary:** `PASS`, `PASS WITH LIMITS`, `REVISE`, `UNRESOLVED`, `REJECT`

## 0. Executive decision

The concept has a defensible Washington legal chassis, but only after separating five things the original intuition can easily collapse:

1. the participant's factual assertion;
2. the participant's deliberate adoption of that assertion under oath or affirmation;
3. the notary's certification of the **act**, not the assertion's truth;
4. the elements, proof, jurisdiction, and discretion required for any criminal case; and
5. DGG's separate editorial, recording, publication, campaign, privacy, and research obligations.

The strongest current version is a **private, voluntary, participant-authored, fixed factual record** signed and sworn or affirmed before an independent Washington notary. Washington's false-swearing text is the strongest possible criminal backstop because the legislature defines an oath as authorized by law when an authorized person administers it. That conclusion is textually strong, but no reported Washington appellate decision located by this audit applies the amended definition to a voluntary private political-media record. First-degree perjury is not a dependable public claim because no square authority makes an ordinary private notarial session an “official proceeding.”

The recommended first live pilot is therefore narrower than every route current law may permit:

- in person in Washington;
- participant or participant's counsel writes every substantive answer and qualification;
- DGG supplies preapproved questions, sources, formatting, and an objective-error process, but does not coauthor an adopted answer;
- an independent notary enters only after the exact record is frozen;
- no unscripted substantive discussion occurs while the oath is active;
- only a completed, separately publication-authorized package is eligible for release;
- no public invitee, “declined,” or “no response” page is used until audience testing shows that the label does not imply dishonesty or fear of prosecution; and
- remote and interstate expansion occurs only after separate written approval of the exact workflow and the participant-location law.

This is a **go for synthetic dry runs and counsel review**, not a go for live use.

## 1. Audit method and controlling rule

Eight supplied project documents and the current public repository were treated as research leads, not as authority. Consequential propositions were decomposed into elements and checked in this order:

1. current statute, enacted session law, and regulation;
2. reported controlling or persuasive cases, including adverse cases;
3. official forms and agency guidance;
4. secondary sources only for search closure or unresolved interpretation;
5. project documents only for design conflicts and provenance.

An operational claim receives `PASS` only if both the legal proposition and every stated condition are supported. A route can be legally available yet still receive `REVISE` as a first-pilot design because validity, proof, privacy, audience harm, and interstate administration are separate questions.

### 1.1 Claim-publication test

A public legal claim is eligible only if all six terms are satisfied:

\[
\text{Publishable claim} = A \cap F \cap L \cap C \cap X \cap R
\]

where:

- \(A\) = current authority supports the exact words;
- \(F\) = the required operational facts are documented;
- \(L\) = adverse and limiting law is disclosed where material;
- \(C\) = the claim is consistent across the form, script, status page, and training;
- \(X\) = no unresolved interstate, recording, campaign, or privacy gate changes the result; and
- \(R\) = the required counsel and notary review is complete.

Failure of one term means revise, withhold, or stop. The equation is an audit rule, not a statement of legal doctrine.

## 3. Core Washington argument that survives audit

### 3.1 The legal act

Washington authorizes a notary to administer an oath or affirmation and to take a verification on oath or affirmation. A verification is the individual's declaration that a statement in a record is true. Personal appearance, identity determination, a contemporaneous certificate, and a journal entry are part of the notarial chassis. See [RCW 5.28.010](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=5.28.010), [chapter 42.45 RCW](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=42.45&full=true), and [chapter 308-30 WAC](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=308-30&full=true).

For the fixed-record route, the record should state its sworn character, the participant should explicitly understand and intend that character, the notary should administer an oral oath or affirmation, and the notary should append the contemporaneous jurat. Washington permits less ritual in some circumstances, but an explicit ceremony avoids a factual dispute about whether the signer was actually sworn. *State v. Lewis*, 85 Wn.2d 769, 539 P.2d 677 (1975), is relevant only to that narrow formality issue; it does not validate the DGG design or establish an official proceeding.

### 3.2 Why false swearing is the strongest possible backstop

[RCW 9A.72.040](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.72.040) applies when a person makes a statement the person knows is false under an oath required or authorized by law. [RCW 9A.72.010(5)](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.72.010) now says an oath is authorized by law when administered by a person authorized by state, tribal, or federal law to administer oaths. The legislature added that route in [1995 Washington Laws chapter 285, section 30](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/biennium/1995-96/Pdf/Bills/Session%20Laws/House/1557-S2.SL.pdf).

That amendment matters because *State v. Hovrud*, 60 Wn. App. 573, 805 P.2d 250 (1991), rejected a private-policy oath under the narrower prior definition. The amendment superseded the statutory premise of that holding. The remaining caution is application: no reported Washington appellate case located in this audit applies the amended route to a voluntary private political-media record. The proper description is therefore **“possible false-swearing exposure under strong statutory text”**, not “settled prosecution,” “enforceable truth,” or “guaranteed crime.”

### 3.3 Why first-degree perjury is not the product

First-degree perjury additionally requires a materially false statement in an “official proceeding.” [RCW 9A.72.020](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.72.020). The definition includes proceedings before authorized evidence-takers and expressly mentions a notary, but the located applications have a governmental, judicial, formal deposition, licensing, or probate connection. See *In re Recall of Pearsall-Stipek*, 141 Wn.2d 756, 10 P.3d 1034 (2000); *State v. Jacobson*, 74 Wn. App. 715, 876 P.2d 916 (1994); and *In re Estate of Starkel*, 134 Wn. App. 1, 138 P.3d 519 (2006).

A purely private publication format also creates a difficult materiality question: what legally cognizable proceeding could the statement affect? DGG should neither manufacture a nominal proceeding nor connect the format to government to obtain a stronger criminal label. That move would raise new state-action, campaign, coercion, and validity problems.

### 3.4 Proof must remain separate from publication judgment

The process records what was said, but does not adjudicate truth or guilt. *State v. Arquette*, 178 Wn. App. 273, 314 P.3d 426 (2013), applies a heightened corroboration rule to perjury. *State v. Olson*, 92 Wn.2d 134, 594 P.2d 1337 (1979), protects a literally true answer even when evasive; ambiguity and the speaker's understanding matter. [RCW 9A.72.050](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.72.050) supplies a narrow inconsistent-statement route for material statements in official proceedings. No published Washington appellate criminal opinion located by this audit squarely resolves whether the traditional corroboration rule applies to standalone false swearing.

DGG's public vocabulary should therefore be “supported,” “contradicted,” “unresolved,” “qualified,” or “corrected” under a disclosed evidence rubric—not “guilty,” “perjury,” or “proved false” merely because a reviewer disagrees.

## 5. Counsel-ready first-pilot procedure

### 5.1 Phase 0 — synthetic only

Run the complete process with invented questions, trained actors, synthetic identity data, and a nonoperative mock certificate. Test role separation, frozen-record comparison, stop authority, interruption restart, consent layers, access controls, hashes, correction links, derivative takedown, and comprehension. Do not describe Phase 0 as a notarized public-figure pilot.

### 5.2 Invitation and question admission

Before acceptance, give the same viewpoint-neutral packet to every invitee:

- exact questions, time windows, defined terms, and source packet;
- participation, item-level decline, qualification, “I do not know,” counsel, pause, and withdrawal choices;
- a plain statement that the notary does not certify substantive truth;
- separate terms for the notarial act, DGG recording, editing, publication, reuse, data retention, and corrections;
- a statement that possible law depends on facts and independent actors and that DGG cannot promise prosecution or immunity; and
- no public nonparticipation consequence during the initial pilot.

Admit only narrow, falsifiable, sourceable factual propositions within the participant's personal or documented knowledge. Exclude opinion, prediction, motive, compound questions, disputed legal conclusions, and accusations whose truthful answer would predictably require privileged, classified, sealed, victim-identifying, medical, or protected information.

### 5.3 Green — unsworn preparation

The participant or participant's counsel writes the answer. DGG may identify an objective transcription or formatting error and ask the participant to resolve ambiguity, but may not improve, paraphrase, select, or coauthor the substance. The participant may qualify, decline, or state lack of knowledge without penalty. All editorial conversation remains explicitly unsworn.

### 5.4 Amber — freeze and compare

Assign a unique record ID and version. Freeze the complete proposed CSR, exhibits, signature fields, and approved certificate form. The participant confirms that the fixed record—not a prior draft, prompt, or conversation—is the exact statement being adopted. DGG stops substantive discussion. The notary independently confirms identity, willingness, authority, the record presented, and the appropriate act.

### 5.5 Red — sworn act

The notary controls the ceremony, administers the approved oath or affirmation, observes the participant's signature, and completes the contemporaneous jurat and journal. DGG asks no new substantive question. If the participant needs to change an answer, stop and return to an unsworn new version; never improvise a rewrite while treating the original oath as covering it.

### 5.6 Close, correct, and publish

Before closing, offer an immediate opportunity to correct, qualify, retract, decline, or consult counsel without promising that any change is a statutory defense. Reconcile the record ID, version, certificate, and authorized DGG production copy. Hash the public-eligible artifacts. Publish only after a separate authorization and prepublication review. Show the complete CSR, certificate, method and limitations, context/source sheet, and every correction; derivative clips must travel with correction status.

## 6. Accident, correction, rewriting, and interstate response

| Trigger | Immediate action | Record consequence | Restart / counsel gate |

| 2026 remote session becomes free-form oral-only | Stop the sworn lane | Treat it as incomplete for DGG's sworn-record purposes | Later signed verification is a new record, not retroactive conversion |

| Suspected inaccurate answer | Do not accuse, coach, threaten, negotiate referral, or demand a replacement | Preserve exact record and evidence; restrict publication pending review | Offer qualification, unknown, correction, decline, and counsel; media/legal review before any public conclusion |

| Substantive error found after notarization | Do not overwrite, detach, transplant, or reuse the certificate | Original remains immutable; create linked correction/superseding version | New signature, oath/affirmation, certificate, and journal entry when the correction is itself sworn |
| Misleading derivative or error discovered after publication | Preserve evidentiary copy; promptly disable or annotate affected derivatives | Visible correction must follow clips and syndication where operationally possible | Media/privacy counsel controls notice, correction, and preservation |
| PII, credential material, KBA data, journal entry, or notarial archive is exposed | Restrict access; do not further reproduce | Incident log and preservation copy stay outside the public repository | Privacy/security counsel and the lawful custodian direct response |
| Complaint, subpoena, warrant, regulator inquiry, threatened claim, or credible criminal allegation | Stop ordinary deletion and editing | Activate a scoped legal hold; preserve chain of custody | Route to counsel; DGG staff and notary do not freelance a response |
| Campaign/candidate personnel influence question substance, audience, timing, targeting, clips, or distribution | Stop publication | Preserve contact and decision facts outside the public repository | Election and entity counsel classify the activity before resumption |

No “fix” may involve backdating, replacing the original in place, moving a certificate to another record, editing Git history to hide an incident, instructing a notary to alter a journal, or promising that a correction eliminates liability.

## 7. Interstate analysis: six layers, never one shortcut

| Layer | Controlling question | What a valid Washington act does **not** decide |
|---|---|---|
| Notarial authority | Where is the notary, what commission/endorsements/provider apply, and is the act permitted? | It does not establish the participant's truth or criminal liability |
| Recognition | Will the receiving jurisdiction give the certificate notarial effect? | Recognition does not import Washington substantive criminal law |
| Recording/privacy/publicity | What law applies where each person is located and where publication/use occurs? | Statutory RON recording is not editorial consent |
| Territorial criminal jurisdiction | Did the offense occur wholly/partly in Washington, cause the required Washington effect, or fit an express extraterritorial route? | A Washington venue recital is not consent to criminal jurisdiction |
| Evidence and proof | Can the exact record, oath, identity, knowledge, and falsity be authenticated and proved under applicable rules? | A seal is not proof of the assertion's truth |
| Interstate process | Is there a valid charge/warrant and a constitutional/statutory surrender path? | “Possible” does not mean automatic, likely, or appropriate to threaten |

[RCW 9A.04.030](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.04.030) supplies Washington's territorial rules. Its express subsection for specified chapter 5.50 declarations is not a catch-all for every remotely notarized record. A remote signer should receive no boilerplate saying the person “submits to Washington criminal jurisdiction.”

The chapter 5.50 route itself applies only when Washington law independently requires or permits a sworn declaration and the declarant instead uses the authorized unsworn form. It is not a private jurisdiction-selection clause. No reported Washington decision located by this audit applies it to a purely private DGG declaration.

Extradition comes much later. The constitutional fugitive route, [18 U.S.C. § 3182](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3182&num=0&edition=prelim), and cases including *Hyatt v. People ex rel. Corkran*, 188 U.S. 691 (1903), *Strassheim v. Daily*, 221 U.S. 280 (1911), and *Puerto Rico v. Branstad*, 483 U.S. 219 (1987), answer different presence and process questions. A never-present person is not a constitutional fugitive under *Hyatt*. The actual asylum state's Uniform Criminal Extradition Act provision governs any discretionary nonfugitive surrender; [RCW 10.88.250](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=10.88.250) governs only when Washington is the asylum state and does not compel Pennsylvania, Arizona, Florida, or another state. None of these authorities supports a DGG promise that a remote guest will be extradited.

## 8. Pennsylvania: useful comparator, not a Washington substitute

Pennsylvania notarial law can support a remotely or personally signed verification and can recognize a sister-state notarial act. That is useful for identity, formal adoption, provenance, and correction architecture.

The criminal-law gateway is different:

- [18 Pa.C.S. § 4902](https://www.palegis.us/statutes/consolidated/view-statute%26txtType%3DPDF%26ttl%3D18%26div%3D00.%26chpt%3D49%26sctn%3D2%26subsctn%3D0) requires a materially false statement in an official proceeding.
- [18 Pa.C.S. § 4903(a)](https://www.palegis.us/statutes/consolidated/view-statute%26txtType%3DPDF%26ttl%3D18%26div%3D00.%26chpt%3D49%26sctn%3D3%26subsctn%3D0) covers an official proceeding or intent to mislead a public servant.
- Section 4903(b) covers another sworn false statement only if law **requires** it to be sworn before an authorized person. DGG's voluntary choice does not itself create that requirement.
- [18 Pa.C.S. § 4904](https://www.palegis.us/statutes/consolidated/view-statute%26txtType%3DPDF%26ttl%3D18%26div%3D00.%26chpt%3D49%26sctn%3D4%26subsctn%3D0) concerns authorities or a law-authorized false-statement notice, not a privately invented warning.

Pennsylvania is therefore a credible **integrity/provenance implementation** and a weak private criminal-deterrence backbone. Do not forum-shop for a harsher label. The better “something else” is a strong participant declaration, exact versioning, source disclosure, independent fact-checking, calibrated publication, and a durable correction chain.

Stat. §§ 837.011-.012](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0800-0899%2F0837%2F0837.html) require materiality and an official-proceeding or other-official-matter nexus; [§ 910.005](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0900-0999%2F0910%2FSections%2F0910.005.html) independently governs territory. | **Strong RON mechanics; unresolved private-offense fit** |

Neither comparator solves the core design problem without creating its own untested offense and territorial questions. The defensible innovation is the procedure, not a search for the jurisdiction with the most intimidating statute.

### 8.2 Recording law follows a separate map

Washington generally requires all-party consent for a private conversation, and an announcement counts only when the announcement itself is recorded. See [RCW 9.73.030](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9.73.030). *State v. Fowler*, 157 Wn.2d 387, 139 P.3d 342 (2006), generally treats interception as occurring where the recording is made, while agency and governmental involvement can change the analysis. A cloud workflow may involve the participant device, notary device, DGG recorder, platform, server, and subprocessors in different places.

The conservative control is written consent before any capture plus a recorded oral roll call identifying every person, recording, purpose, retention period, access class, editing/publication permission, and withdrawal limit. Stop and re-consent if a participant, location, recorder, or purpose changes. Required RON capture does not authorize DGG's separate recording or publication.

## 9. Publication, defamation, and nonparticipant status

The current repository overstates one Washington defamation proposition. Federal cases such as *Milkovich* and *Masson* describe constitutional and substantial-truth constraints; they do not create Washington tort law. Under *Mohr v. Grant*, 153 Wn.2d 812, 108 P.3d 768 (2005), Washington recognizes a false implication created by material omitted facts when the omitted facts would contradict the publication's false impression. But *Yeakey v. Hearst Communications, Inc.*, 156 Wn. App. 787, 234 P.3d 332 (2010), states that Washington does not recognize defamation by implication based only on the negative implication of entirely true statements.

That correction does **not** make status pages prudent. A status can still be actionable or harmful if it is false, materially incomplete, presented with a misleading headline or edited context, attributes an unexpressed reason, creates a provably false implication through omission, invades privacy, uses identity beyond consent, or is governed by another jurisdiction's law. Public-figure actual malice is a fault rule, not permission to publish what DGG knows or seriously doubts is misleading.

For the initial pilot:

1. do not publish the invitee universe, no-response list, or comparison scoreboard;
2. preregister a blinded audience test of whether exact neutral labels imply dishonesty, guilt, or fear of perjury;
3. require a comprehension threshold and a maximum adverse-inference threshold approved in advance;
4. publish null and adverse results; and
5. if the gate later passes, distinguish explicit decline from no response, retain dated proof, and place the no-inference notice with equal visual prominence.

### 9.1 Campaign and personality-right corrections

One supplied counsel-facing draft says Washington independent-expenditure reporting begins when expenditures reach the $100 amount printed in RCW 29B.25.120. Current [WAC 390-05-400](https://app.leg.wa.gov/WAC/default.aspx?cite=390-05-400), effective January 1, 2026, adjusts subsection (2)'s reporting threshold to **$1,000**. That correction does not classify DGG's activity as an independent expenditure and does not resolve registration, attribution, electioneering-communication, sponsor-identification, coordination, in-kind, or other reporting rules. It means no operative document should repeat the obsolete $100 figure as current law.

Washington personality-right law also needs precise wording. [RCW 63.60.050](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=63.60.050) recognizes written or oral, express or implied consent. [RCW 63.60.070](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=63.60.070) broadly exempts authentic political, newsworthy, public-interest, comment, criticism, and specified media uses, subject to its terms and the rule against falsely claiming endorsement. DGG should still require a specific written release for recording, editing, clips, syndication, fundraising, reuse, and corrections because it creates a clear production license and allocates uses beyond the minimum tort analysis.

The 2026 amendments added “forged digital likeness” to liability provisions without using the same term throughout the existing exemption text. Any synthetic recreation, material voice/image alteration, or endorsement-like promotional use therefore requires separate counsel review; do not assume that an authentic-news exemption automatically resolves manipulated-media liability.

### 9.2 Witness-law ambiguity

DGG should not tell participants that witness tampering or intimidation law is categorically irrelevant. [RCW 9A.72.010(4)](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=9A.72.010) expressly includes a notary or person taking testimony in the “official proceeding” definition. The perjury audit still finds no square case making an ordinary private DGG verification an official proceeding, but that uncertainty cuts against reassurance as well as against threat-based marketing. Any benefit, threat, retaliation, contact with a complainant, or effort to change/preserve testimony-like information should trigger counsel review under the exact collateral statute and facts.

## 10. Research and mathematical integrity

### 10.1 What the behavioral evidence supports

The behavioral literature supports a testable mechanism, not a conclusion about DGG:

- Zickfeld and colleagues' systematic meta-analysis screened 20,988 records and synthesized 445 effects from 121 articles and 91,683 participants in 33 countries. Honesty-directed oaths/pledges had a small average association with more honest behavior, **Hedges's g=.27, 95% CI [.19,.36]**, with heterogeneity and publication-bias concerns.
- Their later preregistered megastudy compared 21 oath variants plus control in 21,506 U.S. and U.K. participants using an incentivized tax-reporting task. Ten variants survived multiplicity adjustment; the effective wordings improved honesty by roughly 4.5 to 8.5 percentage points. Wording mattered, and experts did not reliably predict the winners.
- Pennycook and Rand internally meta-analyzed 20 experiments with 26,863 participants. Accuracy prompts improved sharing discernment primarily by reducing intended sharing of false headlines. This is not a notarial or politician study.
- Walter and colleagues' political fact-checking meta-analysis synthesized 30 studies and 20,963 participants and reported an average improvement, **d=.29**, with weaker effects in important political/campaign conditions.
- Walter and Tukachinsky's continued-influence meta-analysis synthesized 32 studies and 6,527 participants and found a small residual misinformation influence after correction.

The exact DGG package has not been tested. Its effect could arise from advance notice, extra deliberation, selection into participation, source review, accuracy salience, public commitment, legal formality, or editing and correction. It could also increase trust without increasing accuracy. The notarial condition must therefore be compared with an ordinary interview and a nonlegal accuracy commitment using substantively identical content.

## 11. Deconstructive, reconstructive, and middle-out audit

### 11.1 Deconstructive

The original intuition fails if expressed as any of these shortcuts:

- notarized therefore true;
- sworn therefore perjury;
- Washington notary therefore Washington criminal jurisdiction;
- possible charge therefore extradition;
- declined therefore afraid to tell the truth;
- correction therefore immunity; or
- research average therefore predicted politician behavior.

Each shortcut drops at least one legal or causal element. The audit should preserve the historical development as provenance while quarantining these claims from operative copy.

### 11.2 Reconstructive

The durable argument does not need the shortcuts. It combines:

- notary law for deliberate adoption, identity procedure, certificate, and provenance;
- false-swearing law as a possible, carefully qualified backstop;
- participant authorship and exact frozen wording for knowledge and meaning;
- research methods for preregistration, blinded coding, falsification, null-result publication, and uncertainty;
- information-security methods for least privilege, separated custodians, hashes, access logs, and incident response; and
- media ethics for complete context, symmetrical criteria, corrections, and refusal neutrality.

The civic product is a better factual record and correction system. Criminal enforcement is neither a required outcome nor a success metric.

### 11.3 Middle-out

Audit from the smallest consequential unit: one answer in one version by one identified adopter at one time. For that unit, link upward to the exact authority and operational predicate and downward to every place the claim appears—notice, script, certificate, page label, clip, correction, metric, and repository entry. A downstream artifact may not use broader language than its upstream evidence supports.

The atomic chain is:

> question → source boundary → participant-authored answer → knowledge/qualification → frozen version → deliberate oath → notarial certificate → custody/provenance → independent factual review → contextual publication → correction lineage

If any link changes, the claim returns to review. This process is “new” only as a combination; each control is borrowed from established legal, research, records, and safety disciplines.

## 12. Systematic-review process audit

The audit searched the current Washington code and administrative code, session laws, DOL materials, reported Washington oath/perjury/territorial/defamation cases, federal constitutional and interstate sources, Pennsylvania statutes and rules, and original empirical publications. Search closure was defined claim by claim: a lane closed only after it found direct authority, adverse authority, or documented that no square reported case was located.

Important negative findings are not proof of absence:

- no reported Washington appellate case located applies the post-1995 oath definition to a voluntary private political-media CSR;
- no reported Washington case located makes ordinary private notarization alone an official proceeding;
- no published Washington appellate criminal case located squarely applies the traditional corroboration rule to standalone false swearing;
- no direct behavioral study located tests politicians using DGG's notarial architecture; and
- no source supports a probability estimate for charge, conviction, or extradition in this setting.

These negative results must remain visible. Counsel should rerun the searches immediately before live use and after the 2027 statute/rules take effect.

## 13. Robustness and falsifiers

The recommendation survives the following adverse assumptions because the project is not supposed to depend on them:

- first-degree perjury is unavailable;
- false-swearing jurisdiction over an interstate guest is unavailable;
- no prosecutor ever files a case;
- an accuracy commitment performs as well as notarization;
- many participants qualify, say unknown, or decline;
- audience trust does not increase; and
- corrections reveal mistakes by every political viewpoint.

The project should stop or remove the sworn layer if any of these falsifiers occurs:

- controlling authority rejects the false-swearing theory on materially identical facts;
- the notary or counsel cannot approve the exact record/certificate/workflow;
- viewers materially believe the notary certified truth despite the best tested disclosure;
- nonparticipant labels create an unacceptable dishonesty inference;
- participants cannot freely decline, qualify, consult counsel, or stop;
- DGG/campaign involvement compromises independent authorship or editorial classification;
- restricted notarial or identity material cannot be segregated; or
- the sworn layer adds no benefit over a lower-risk accuracy commitment on the preregistered outcomes.

## 16. Updating and repository rule

Before any live pilot, recheck every authority whose effective date or guidance may have changed, rerun the no-square-case searches, read the exact pushed files back from GitHub, and record the commit SHA reviewed. On or after 2027-01-01, rerun the Washington RON analysis from the final statute, WAC, DOL guidance, provider workflow, and certificate form rather than copying the future-law conclusion forward.

Public GitHub artifacts may coordinate legal research, blank procedures, decisions, and synthetic tests. The repository is not the notarial archive, participant file, incident system, privileged counsel workspace, or legal hold.

### 16.1 Public-record and privilege caution

Public visibility does not create attorney-client privilege and ordinarily destroys the confidentiality on which privilege depends. Counsel advice and the client facts supplied for advice should remain in an approved confidential system with access limited to those necessary for the representation.

A private repository is not automatically a Washington Public Records Act repository. But records on private accounts can become agency records when created or used in an official capacity, and a private nonprofit can be analyzed as the functional equivalent of an agency under a fact-intensive governmental-function, funding, control, and creation test. See *Nissen v. Pierce County*, 183 Wn.2d 863, 357 P.3d 45 (2015), and *Horvath v. DBIA Services*, Washington Supreme Court No. 103339-7 (Dec. 18, 2025). Before government funding, delegated functions, incumbent-official participation, or agency collaboration, counsel should classify the repository and impose the correct retention, disclosure, and account-use rules.

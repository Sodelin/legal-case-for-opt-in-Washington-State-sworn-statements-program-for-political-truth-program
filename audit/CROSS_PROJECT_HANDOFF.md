## 1. Merge rule

The packet builder should not merge a legal proposition merely because it appears in a source draft, older printout, canonical memorandum, or this handoff. For each consequential sentence, record:

- claim ID;
- exact public wording;
- primary authority and effective date;
- required factual/technical predicates;
- adverse authority or unresolved gap;
- every form, script, page, clip, and training location affected;
- counsel/notary owner; and
- acceptance-test evidence.

If two project documents conflict, adopt the narrower public claim and the lower-risk first-pilot procedure until a written decision resolves the conflict.

## 3. Judge/red-team questions

The judge lane should issue a written finding on each question, identify the factual assumption that controls, and distinguish a doctrinal failure from a prudent design restriction.

### Washington criminal/notary

1. Does the post-1995 text actually supersede `Hovrud` for this exact participant-authored CSR, or does strict construction leave a private-purpose limit?
2. What is the strongest textual and structural argument that “a proceeding heard before” a notary excludes an ordinary private verification, despite the express notary reference?
3. If the private session is not an official proceeding, can RCW 9A.72.060's “same proceeding” retraction protect a false-swearing correction at all?
4. Does any Washington appellate authority squarely apply a corroboration rule to standalone false swearing? If not, is the packet candid about the gap?
5. Is the exact CSR language enough to show awareness and intent that the record be represented as sworn, without relying on theatrical ritual?
6. Does the independent notary have any direct beneficial interest, promotional role, unauthorized document/certificate-selection role, or outcome-contingent compensation?

### Remote/interstate

7. In a 2026 RON, which exact act or offense element is committed by the out-of-state signer in Washington, apart from the notary's own location?
8. Does any project language improperly turn chapter 5.50 into a private jurisdiction-selection clause?
9. Is the person a constitutional fugitive, or a never-present person for whom the actual asylum state's discretionary statute controls?
10. Where does each device or service acquire the communication; which state recording rule applies; and does any agency relationship alter the conflicts result?
11. Does Arizona's current “whether or not” language overcome `Franzi`, and has a current citator found a post-amendment private-RON holding?
12. Does Florida's notarial-situs rule affect only the act, or also an element under sections 837.011-.012 and territorial section 910.005?

### Media/campaign/safety

13. Can a literal decline/no-response label, in the proposed visual context, create a provably false implication through omitted facts or a highly offensive false light?
14. What measurable audience result would falsify the claim that the status disclaimer prevents an adverse inference?
15. Is DGG a bona fide media entity, campaign actor, committee, corporation, tax-exempt entity, government contractor, or functional equivalent of an agency for the channel at issue?
16. Did a candidate, committee, or representative request, materially involve itself in, or share nonpublic plans about content, audience, timing, targeting, clips, or distribution?
17. Does any authentic-use personality-right exemption apply, and does a synthetic/forged alteration fall outside the currently worded exemption?
18. Does any participant-facing statement use prosecution, referral, publication, or status consequences as leverage?

### Evidence and research

19. Can a blinded reviewer identify the exact assertion, source boundary, qualification, adopted version, and correction without seeing treatment condition or political identity?
20. Is audience trust calibrated to objectively coded accuracy, or does the notarial badge increase raw trust regardless of truth?
21. Does a nonlegal accuracy commitment perform as well as the sworn condition? If yes, what remaining benefit justifies the extra legal/privacy burden?
22. Will null, asymmetric, and adverse findings be published under the preregistration?

Do not ask counsel to approve an abstraction. Provide:

- DGG legal name, entity type, incorporation, owners/directors, editorial controller, funding, contracts, tax status, and government relationships;
- ordinary publication history, distribution channels, paid promotion, covered elections/offices, and FEC/PDC registrations;
- all candidate/committee contacts and who proposed questions, sources, timing, audience, clips, and distribution;
- exact participant/notary locations and every recording/capture/storage location;
- notary commission, endorsements, provider, contract, fee, conflict check, certificate, journal, and archive workflow;
- exact questions, sources, participant-authored CSR, oath, notice, release, correction terms, retention schedule, and public layout;
- data-flow and custody diagram, least-privilege roles, incident owner, and legal-hold process; and
- preregistration, sampling frame, codebook, power analysis, adverse-outcome thresholds, and publication commitment.

## 5. Update-packet protocol

Each future project packet should enter GitHub through a dated branch or pull request and include:

1. source name, version, date, origin project, and SHA-256;
2. whether it is authority, advice, draft language, evidence, or historical provenance;
3. changed claim IDs and the reason for change;
4. effective dates and a current-law recheck;
5. conflicts with the paralegal audit, canonical memorandum, control packet, or judge findings;
6. public-safety review; and
7. a disposition: adopt, adopt with limits, quarantine as history, supersede, or reject.

Never resolve a substantive conflict by silently overwriting the older conclusion. Keep the prior commit, state the falsifier or new authority, and link the replacement decision.

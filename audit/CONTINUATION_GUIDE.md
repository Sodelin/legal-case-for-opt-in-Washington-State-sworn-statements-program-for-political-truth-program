# Continuation guide

This guide lets a future contributor continue the project without access to the originating conversations.

## Canonical files

1. `memorandum/DGG_WASHINGTON_SWORN_STATEMENT_MEMORANDUM.md` - controlling legal and strategic memorandum.
2. `research/report-source.md` - source ledger and legal research record.
3. `research/GAP_MATRIX.md` - consequential-claim audit.
4. `operations/DGG_PILOT_CONTROL_PACKET.md` - Phase 0 operating controls.
5. `audit/PROJECT_CONTROL.md` - mission, red lines, and publication gates.

Supplemental files must not silently displace these.

## Critical facts not to lose

- The format is private and voluntary.
- The canonical object is a fixed factual-answer record, not the entire interview.
- False swearing and first-degree perjury have different elements.
- The exact private-media false-swearing application is unresolved.
- First-degree perjury is not a dependable public theory.
- Before January 1, 2027, a remote pilot should be record-based.
- Beginning January 1, 2027, Washington law expressly adds a standalone remote oral-oath path, subject to a fresh check.
- The Washington notary must remain physically in Washington for a Washington remote act.
- A generic Zoom call is not the compliant RON system.
- The notary verifies the act and identity, not truth.
- Notarial validity and Washington criminal jurisdiction are separate.
- Extradition is not an automatic interstate-compact mechanism.
- A correction creates a new dated record and does not erase the original.
- Refusal or silence must not be presented as evidence of dishonesty.

2. Obtain First Amendment/media/privacy review.
3. Obtain participant-state review for any nonresident.
4. Audit one named RON provider against current WAC requirements.
5. Shepardize or KeyCite every case before reliance.
6. Recheck RCW, WAC, and DOL guidance after January 1, 2027.
7. Obtain an IRB/HRPP determination before generalizable audience research.
8. Create a private records-security and incident-response plan outside the public repository.

## Safe update sequence

1. Recheck current primary law.
2. Add new authority to `research/report-source.md`.
3. Update the relevant row in `research/GAP_MATRIX.md`.
4. Revise the canonical memorandum only if the source record supports the change.
5. Update operations controls.
6. Recalculate canonical checksums.
7. Record the change and its reason.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- treating a statutory argument as a holding;
- using evidence-rule cases to establish criminal elements;
- importing another state's law by analogy;
- describing a notarial certificate as fact verification;
- using public participant data, identity material, journals, or raw notarial recordings in the repository;
- promising criminal consequences;
- launching before written counsel and notary approval.

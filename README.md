# Voluntary Political Sworn-Statement Pilot

This repository develops a legal and operational memorandum for **Digital Ground Game** concerning a private, voluntary, opt-in format that separates narrowly defined sworn factual statements from ordinary unsworn political commentary.

## Status

**Research and legal audit in progress.** Nothing in this repository is legal advice, and no draft should be described as establishing automatic perjury liability, nationwide jurisdiction, or guaranteed enforcement.

## Repository map

- `archive/input-drafts/` — source drafts preserved exactly as received; these are not the final legal position.
- `memo/` — canonical memorandum and executive brief (to be added after primary-law verification).
- `research/` — claim-to-source ledger, authority table, jurisdiction comparison, and open-issue log.
- `pilot/` — operational forms, scripts, correction procedures, and safeguards after legal review.
- `audit/` — citation checks, red-team findings, and reproducibility records.

## Controlling design assumptions

The proposed pilot is private, voluntary, nonpartisan, and record-centered. A notary administers the notarial act and verifies identity and execution; the notary does **not** certify the factual truth of a participant’s statements. Criminal consequences, if any, depend on the exact facts, statutory elements, jurisdiction, and prosecutorial discretion.

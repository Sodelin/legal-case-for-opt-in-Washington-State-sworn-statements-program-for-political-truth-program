## Design disposition

The public-product proposal was changed from a sworn statement to a deliberately **non-sworn Claim Record**. The new proposal omits an oath, affirmation, jurat, notarial verification, penalty-of-perjury clause, government filing, truth badge, refusal list, and project-created criminal-enforcement pathway.

This change responds directly to the stated participant-safety goal. It does **not** claim legal immunity or promise that public speech is risk-free. Ordinary publication, privacy, contract, campaign, recording, and other law still apply. Any real-person use therefore remains subject to current Washington counsel, editorial, privacy, security, accessibility, methods, and campaign review.

# Can an exact, source-linked claim record improve clarity and correction?

**Document ID:** DGG-PUB-000  
**Version:** 0.1 candidate  
**Authority cutoff:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** **PUBLICLY VISIBLE REVIEW DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PROMOTIONAL RELEASE, RECRUITMENT, CANDIDATE OUTREACH, OR LIVE USE**

> This is a research proposal under internal legal and methods review. It is not a government program, a live candidate project, legal advice, or a truth certification.

## The proposal

Political interviews mix checkable facts with predictions, opinions, values, advocacy, humor, and rhetoric. This project asks a narrow question: could a willing speaker make a few suitable factual commitments easier to inspect by placing their exact words, knowledge basis, cited sources, version, and later corrections into a stable record?

If a later phase were approved, the speaker would retain three meaningful choices:

1. put a narrow factual statement into a sworn record;
2. discuss the subject in ordinary unsworn conversation; or
3. qualify the answer, say “I do not know,” or decline the question or format.

DGG would draw and publish no adverse inference from nonparticipation. Because third-party political reactions cannot be controlled, Phase 0 publishes no invitation, participation, refusal, or nonresponse status.

## What the notary would—and would not—do

The signer declares the final statement true under oath. The notary checks required identity and signature, administers the oath or affirmation, and completes the notarial certificate.

The notary does **not** investigate the source material, decide whether the political claim is true, predict a crime, or become DGG's spokesperson. The record would preserve provenance and an exact commitment; it would not carry a “true” badge.

## A fictional example

> **Prompt:** “As of 5:00 p.m. Pacific time on August 1, 2026, did the fictional Pine County Project ledger attached as Source A record exactly three payments from Example Vendor totaling $12,450?”  
> **Selected response:** Yes.  
> **Final statement:** “As of 5:00 p.m. Pacific time on August 1, 2026, the fictional Pine County Project ledger attached as Source A recorded exactly three payments from Example Vendor totaling $12,450.”

The hypothetical record would identify the speaker's stated knowledge basis, the cutoff time, the fictional source bundle, a version ID, and a SHA-256 file fingerprint. That fingerprint can detect changed bytes; it does not prove the claim is true. This example has no real speaker, oath, notary, or legal effect.

## Why test it

The format might reduce quotation disputes, encourage narrower wording, normalize “unknown” and qualification, link sources to an exact statement, and preserve a visible correction history. Controlled oath studies supply a reason to test carefully, but they do not establish results for politicians, voters, public reputation, or notarized interviews.

The defensible claim is **worth testing under safeguards**, not “proven to make politicians honest.” The study must measure harmful effects too: coercion, partisan asymmetry, speaker selection, chilled speech, privacy concern, and the mistaken belief that notarization proves truth.

## Legal limits that must stay attached

- Notarization does not certify factual truth.
- A false answer does not automatically establish perjury or any other crime.
- Possible consequences depend on exact elements, evidence, jurisdiction, constitutional limits, and official discretion.
- Government truth adjudication, candidate badges, censure, complaint burdens, and public nonsigner lists present serious constitutional risk.
- DGG's private-actor status, entity, funding, control relationships, contacts, distribution plan, forms, and data architecture have not yet been verified.

Washington law identifies oath, verification, false-swearing, and perjury questions worth researching. No located controlling case decides that the proposed private political-media format triggers criminal liability. The project must be valuable even if nobody is investigated or prosecuted.

## What Phase 0 permits

The current phase may use only fictional materials without a real signer, oath, or notarial act. A separate private, in-person Washington test using literally true statements about the test itself is a possible future branch only after a reviewed supersession closes the remand and all professional and internal gates are satisfied.

It does not permit a named candidate or public figure, promotional launch, candidate outreach, livestream, remote DGG act, public participation status, truth verdict, or criminal-enforcement claim.

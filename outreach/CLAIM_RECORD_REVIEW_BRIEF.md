# Claim Record — internal design review brief

**Selected non-sworn development; synthetic Phase 0 only.** This is a review explanation, not a participant invitation, cleared external pitch or live form.

A Claim Record keeps a bounded statement, its stated basis and qualifications together. A separate section records an editor's evidence assessment. Corrections identify the version changed, distinguishing the person's statement from the editor's conclusion and old material from current material.

The proposed process observes willingness to document one bounded claim and basis. That is not a validated confidence or honesty measure. The design adds no program oath, notarial verification or penalty-of-perjury mechanism. Effectiveness is unestablished.

The intended organization is separate and open across parties. Legal entity, governance, funding and editorial appointments remain to be determined. DGG is a possible recipient; no partner agreement is inferred.

The version provides no named decline/no-response pages, participation scores or truth badges. Participant wording and editorial findings remain separate. Actual recording, publication, reuse and recruitment require applicable reviews first.

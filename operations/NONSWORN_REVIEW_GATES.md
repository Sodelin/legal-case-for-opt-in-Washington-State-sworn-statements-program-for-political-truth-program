# Non-sworn Claim Record: review before actual use

**Status:** Phase 0 synthetic/tabletop development only. **Primary sources inspected:** September 5, 2026. This implements the intake requested by the [PR #5 review](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/pull/5#issuecomment-5471377748); it is not counsel approval or permission for real-person use.

The proposed operator is a separate organization with participation available across parties. Its entity, officers, tax status and professional reviewers remain unappointed/unverified. DGG is a possible recipient, not an established operator or partner. Roles below are responsibilities to fill, not existing appointments. Synthetic drafting does not require closing these future-use gates.

## 1. One factual intake for each proposed use

The production lead prepares a dated, restricted intake with:

- Initiator, operator, funder, publisher, beneficiaries, ownership/control, candidate/committee relationships and office sought (state/local/judicial/federal).
- Exact record and derivative versions; claim, sources, qualifications, third parties, intended audience, medium, jurisdictions and distribution/election dates.
- All payments, donated services, discounts, production costs, licensing terms, paid placement/boosting and planned organic reuse; aggregate related activity.
- Who selected questions, supplied material, requested changes or influenced findings, audience, timing or placement; relevant staff/vendor campaign connections.
- Each speaker's and recorder's actual location, recording/transcription vendors, consent purposes, data flows, access, retention, deletion and legal-hold arrangements.
- Proposed operator/contracting party, contracts, budget authority, named reviewers and factual support for any claimed exemption.

Every applicable branch receives a written disposition identifying facts, authority, reviewer, date, exact asset/version, conditions and reopening triggers. Missing facts stay open; an unsupported “not applicable” does not close a gate. Confidential intake, consents and participant data stay outside this public repository.

Before actual use, reviewing counsel must recheck current primary law and obtain current later-treatment/citator results for any case-law proposition relied on. The historical sworn memorandum's commercial-citator condition remains unclosed; this intake is not a publication-ready legal opinion. Current Washington adjusted amounts must be checked against [WAC 390-05-400](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=390-05-400), together with the classification-specific statute and any other applicable rule.

## 2. Campaign and government branches

**Washington public resources.** If state officers/employees or state facilities are involved, apply [RCW 42.52.180](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=42.52.180), including its exceptions and subsection (3)'s exclusion of the alternative regime. For other covered public officials/agencies, assess [RCW 29B.45.010](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=29B.45.010), which expressly excludes state officers/employees. Inventory paid staff time, accounts, equipment, offices and lists; record capacity and any specific exception. A cross-party invitation policy does not itself establish an exception. New government involvement reopens this branch.

**Washington campaign classification.** Counsel separately determines contribution, electioneering-communication and other applicable classifications. [RCW 29B.10.160](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=29B.10.160) addresses value, coordination and financed republication; [RCW 29B.10.220](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=29B.10.220) addresses identified candidates, medium, election timing and aggregate value. Their news exclusions require a regularly scheduled news medium, public interest, a controller whose business is that medium, and absence of candidate/political/incidental-committee control. Record predicate evidence separately from conclusions. Before financial commitments or distribution, verify current reporting, disclaimer, aggregation and inflation-adjusted thresholds in writing; do not copy historical dollar amounts.

**Federal candidates/parties.** Assess payment, content and conduct separately under [11 CFR 109.21](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-109/subpart-C/section-109.21); all three are needed for that coordinated-communication classification, subject to applicable exceptions. Record requests, material involvement, campaign discussions, vendor/staff relationships and any substantiated safe harbor. Apply [11 CFR 100.26](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-100/subpart-A/section-100.26) to the actual medium, including Internet placement or promotion for a fee. Organic distribution is not a blanket campaign-law exemption.

For a claimed press exception, establish the actual entity, ownership/control and activity under [11 CFR 100.73](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-100/subpart-C/section-100.73) and [100.132](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-100/subpart-E/section-100.132). Political ownership/control requires assessing their narrower qualifying-news conditions. The [FEC's AO 2008-14 analysis](https://www.fec.gov/updates/ao-2008-14-internet-tv-station-qualifies-for-press-exemption/) is a fact-specific comparison, not this project's exemption.

**Debate/broadcast triggers.** If converted into a federal candidate debate, verify an eligible staging organization, at least two candidates, a structure that does not advance one candidate, and pre-established objective selection criteria under [11 CFR 110.13](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-110/section-110.13). If a licensed broadcaster carries candidate appearances, its reviewer must determine candidate “use,” any bona fide news exemption and resulting equal-opportunity obligations under [47 CFR 73.1941](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-73/subpart-H/section-73.1941). An online interview is not automatically either category.

## 3. Recording and publication branches

**Recording.** Prior written consent for recording, transcription, retention and defined publication purposes is project policy. [RCW 9.73.030](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=9.73.030) covers private communications/conversations and contains distinct announcement and news-gathering provisions; it does not universally prescribe this written multipurpose form. Counsel reviews privacy, actual locations and potentially applicable other jurisdictions before recording. Recording consent and publication permission receive separate decisions. A change in participants, location, vendor or purpose reopens review.

**Complete composite.** Editorial lead and publication counsel review the participant's exact text, editorial finding, linked/source material, identifiable third parties, headline, thumbnail, captions and every clip for defamation, privacy, misleading implication and permissions. Preserve contrary evidence, attribution boundaries and material qualifications. The participant sees the final composite and approves exact attribution/context; this creates no veto over the independent editorial conclusion. Material changes require re-proof. Proposed publicity/advertising/fundraising use of a person's identity requires its own assessment under [RCW 63.60.050](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=63.60.050) and the exceptions in [63.60.070](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=63.60.070).

**Derivative/reuse request.** Require a discrete written classification and appropriate license for the exact asset ID/version/hash, source parent, permitted edits, excerpt/context, destination, audience, term, paid/organic distribution, attribution/disclaimers and correction/takedown duties. Check third-party rights separately. Prior editorial permission does not automatically cover campaign advertising, fundraising or boosting. Link each derivative to the current source; assign correction notification and verification owners. Distinguish controlled copies from outside copies whose removal cannot be guaranteed. Changed use or material edits reopen review.

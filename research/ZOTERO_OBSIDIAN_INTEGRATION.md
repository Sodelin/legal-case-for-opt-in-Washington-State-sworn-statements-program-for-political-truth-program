## Bibliography discipline

The bibliography is not merely a list. Every source should be connected to:

1. the exact claim it supports;
2. the strongest contrary authority;
3. whether the source is controlling, persuasive, or analogical;
4. the date on which current text was checked;
5. the operational document affected by a change.

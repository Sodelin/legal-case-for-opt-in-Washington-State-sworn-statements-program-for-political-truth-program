## 2. Agreements repeated across documents

1. Participation is voluntary and ordinary unsworn discussion remains available (01 §§2–6; 02 §0; 04 §§1.1, 5; 05 §§2, 4; 09 p. 2; 11 §0).
2. The defined, adopted record is distinct from later commentary; the format is not a general oath covering an entire improvised interview (02 §§12–14; 04 §3; 05 §2.2; 06 §14A; 09 pp. 2–3, 8).
3. A notary's act does not certify substantive factual truth, and participation/decline do not establish truth/deception (02 §§4, 15; 04 §§1.3, 3.2, 5.6; 09 pp. 1–3; 11 §§0–1).
4. False swearing, first-degree perjury, territorial criminal jurisdiction, document recognition, and interstate surrender are distinct questions (02 §§2, 9; 04 §§4, 7–8; 06 §§15–19; 09 pp. 4, 20–22; 10 §4.8; 11 §2).
5. No document supplies a directly evaluated Washington sworn-campaign program or a demonstrated effect of this exact protocol on politician accuracy or public calibration (04 §§2.4, 10; 08 §§0–3; 09 pp. 1, 6, 10; 11 §§0, 3).
6. The records are research/design drafts rather than counsel approval, organizational acceptance, or a passed law (04 §0; 06 cover and reliance warning; 09 pp. 1–2, 25; 11 §3 and ending).

Agreement among overlapping drafts is evidence of project continuity, not independent confirmation of the underlying law or effects.

## 4. Open questions the attachments leave for a concrete record

The supplied record does not identify a signed host agreement or counsel opinion; current host legal entity/tax treatment; final question and answer record; approved notarial certificate and vendor; settled nonparticipant publication rule; final custody/access/retention and recording-release documents; state-specific signatory locations; empirical recruitment or results; verified stakeholder acceptance; or a bill with identified sponsor, administering authority, appropriations, enforcement powers, and government-program analysis.

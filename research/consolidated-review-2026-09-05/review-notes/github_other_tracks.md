## What PR #5 actually changes in its separate proposal

The PR #5 explainer introduces a deliberately non-sworn Claim Record: exact question, participant-approved words, basis and qualifications, supporting and contrary sources, a separate publisher assessment, version identification, and linked corrections. Its proposed public product contains no oath, affirmation, jurat, notarial verification, penalty-of-perjury clause, or government filing. It explicitly distinguishes this from legal immunity or a claim that public speech is risk-free. [PR #5 explainer, §§1–2, 9, 13](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/blob/73a036076096c0874478ccfdffdc5e605378765a/outreach/DGG_CLAIM_RECORD_HUMAN_EXPLAINER_v1.0.md).

The explainer's publication rule excludes named invitee, refusal, nonresponse, and withdrawal lists; this is broader than a temporary prohibition pending audience testing. It assigns the participant control over attributed words and the publisher control over questions, evidence assessment, and publication. Its research proposal compares an ordinary interview, an accuracy reminder, and the full non-sworn record. These are proposed operations and study conditions, not observations that the format has worked or been accepted. [PR #5 explainer, §§2, 4, 7, 15–17](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/blob/73a036076096c0874478ccfdffdc5e605378765a/outreach/DGG_CLAIM_RECORD_HUMAN_EXPLAINER_v1.0.md).

The cover note describes the “current idea” as non-sworn. Within the repository, that phrase belongs to this proposed alternative track: the PR description explicitly preserves the sworn candidate separately and says the alternative does not supersede it. The cover note is an unsent draft; these reads establish no outreach delivery or DGG acceptance. [PR #5 cover note](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/blob/73a036076096c0874478ccfdffdc5e605378765a/outreach/DGG_SENDABLE_COVER_NOTE_v1.0.md), [PR #5 scope and requested disposition](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/pull/5).

## What this comparison does and does not establish

The repository contains distinguishable artifacts: a canonical Phase 0 sworn-record control packet, unmerged sworn-candidate development, an unmerged adversarial review, and an unmerged non-sworn alternative. A later filename/date, a document's use of “current,” or a review's “order” wording does not establish that a repository change was accepted. The documents themselves repeatedly distinguish research supplements from implemented edits. [Main control packet](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/blob/3905b470c2ab239cf2aae563e1d7b3a1adb73dd3/operations/DGG_PILOT_CONTROL_PACKET.md), [PR #4](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/pull/4), [PR #5](https://github.com/Sodelin/legal-case-for-opt-in-Washington-State-sworn-statements-program-for-political-truth-program/pull/5).

This comparison does not certify the substantive legal findings in either branch, repeat their empirical claims as established results, choose a product, or express an adoption recommendation. It records competing scopes and the sources' stated review posture. The difference between an oath-based record and a publication-only record is present in the actual texts; reconciling that difference cannot be accomplished by silently calling both the same approved program.

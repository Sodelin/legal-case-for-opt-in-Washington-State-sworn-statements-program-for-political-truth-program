# Source-attributed operational gap map

Scope: descriptive comparison of supplied drafts. This document records what the drafts specify, where their instructions differ, and what remains unspecified. It does not endorse a political proposal, predict organizational acceptance, or establish legal clearance. Proposed controls are attributed to their authors; their inclusion is not an independent conclusion that they are legally sufficient.

Source keys:

- **P02:** *Washington Voluntary Sworn-Format Pilot*, revised pilot packet v2, March 21, 2026; source file 02. Section references use the document's headings.
- **P04:** *Washington Participant-Sworn Statement Protocol*, August 30, 2026; source file 04. This is the detailed operating and incident protocol.
- **P09:** *Washington Voluntary Sworn-Statement Pilot*, September 5, 2026, 25-page PDF; source file 09. Page references use printed page numbers.
- **P11:** *Voluntary Factual Statements: Feasibility*, source file 11. Sections 1–3 and 11–12.

## Narrow current-law cross-check for record custody

The official Washington journal statute specifies ten years after the last act chronicled in the journal, provides an attorney practice-record exception, and places the journal under the notary's direct and exclusive control. These provisions do not supply a retention schedule for the host's separate public website or research dataset. [RCW 42.45.180](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=42.45.180).

The remote recording rule specifies at least ten years after recording, secure access and backup, continuing notary responsibility despite third-party storage, and exclusion of images of the electronic record being notarized from that regulated recording. This supports the drafts' distinction between record classes; it does not authorize publication of them. [WAC 308-30-330](https://app.leg.wa.gov/wac/default.aspx?cite=308-30-330).

## Process and inference notes

The inventory is based on four supplied documents, not an audit of executed contracts, deployed software, participant files, institutional review determinations, or an operating pilot. The current-law check above is limited to custody/retention; the parent review separately addresses substantive legal issues. A document containing an instruction to establish a control is evidence that its author identified that control, not evidence that the control exists. No acceptance, effectiveness, or legality result is inferred from completeness of the written architecture.
